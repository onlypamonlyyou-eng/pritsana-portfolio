The problem

A clinic that couldn't afford a robot, or a bottleneck.

Inspire IVF needed to cut down repetitive patient questions without making people feel handed off to a machine, especially on sensitive topics like cost, personal success rates, or legal questions around surrogacy. The real challenge wasn't building a chatbot; it was designing a system where AI could act naturally, and step aside for a human the moment it mattered, with no gap where a patient could fall through.

THE TENSION

All AI

Feels impersonal on sensitive questions: patients disengage.

All human

Doesn't scale: staff re-answer the same questions all day.

What LUNA had to be

AI that knows exactly when to step aside: no gap, no guessing.

01 · What I built

Six systems, one conversation.

Every part of LUNA was designed around one rule: never guess when a human should know.

01

Human Handoff

7-stage lead status with an instant "claim" action so replies never collide.

02

Tool-Calling Detection

Migrated from keyword matching to structured Tool Calling via the Claude API.

03

Multi-channel Alerts

04

Content Safety Tiering

Legally & medically sensitive topics always defer to a specialist, no exceptions.

05

SLA-aware Timing

Callback promises calculated against real business hours, never a false one.

06

UX & Persona

Quick-reply buttons, responsive widget, and a transparent, consistent AI voice.

Part of the proposed plan

The governance questions I wrote answers for.

Before this next phase could go to leadership for approval, it needed clear, defensible answers: 5 of the 10 I wrote.

01What data can (and can't) the AI use?

Only approved clinic posts and reviewed FAQ files. It's explicitly barred from using its own general medical knowledge to diagnose an individual, or quoting any price not pulled from the approved knowledge base.

02What happens when it doesn't know an answer?

A fixed fallback script hands the conversation to the admin team immediately. It is not allowed to guess.

03When does it hand off to a human?

On a red flag, a personal symptom or financial question, whenever a patient asks for a person, or whenever the AI simply can't find the answer.

04Why not just use a "confidence score" cutoff?

Claude's API doesn't expose a real confidence number, so a "below 85% confidence" rule can't actually be built. I grounded the system strictly to the approved knowledge base instead: same safety outcome, but buildable in reality.

05Who checks the answers, and how often?

5–10% of all conversations are randomly sampled and audited weekly by senior nursing/admin staff.

Next phase · proposed

Scoping the safety-critical expansion.

Beyond the live product, I authored a full governance and deployment plan extending LUNA into a medically safety-critical assistant.

01

Red Flag Protocol

An emergency-detection layer that runs before every other rule: catching phrases like heavy bleeding or severe pain and routing straight to on-call nursing staff via SMS, bypassing the normal queue entirely.

Proposed
02

Governance Framework

Ten answered governance questions covering data use, deferral rules, knowledge-base ownership and audit process, written to survive scrutiny from a clinical and legal review.

Proposed
03

PDPA Consent Flow

A consent banner, explicit opt-in before storing contact details, and a data retention policy for a system handling sensitive fertility health data.

Deploy & ROI Plan

A step-by-step Railway deployment guide, a full cost model from 300 to 10,000 chats a month, and a measurement plan comparing baseline response times against target outcomes.
