The approach

Structure first — rankings follow.

My approach to SEO starts before the content does: information architecture, URL structure and rendering strategy set the ceiling for how well anything can ever rank. I lead with Technical & On-Page SEO woven into the build itself, then layer content strategy and off-page work on top — using GA4 and Search Console to keep decisions grounded in real user behaviour rather than guesses.
